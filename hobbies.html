Hobbies



Drawing:
On occasion I go on a drawing spree, which has been an on-again off-again hobby of mine since my elementary school days. Feel free to check out the results of my latest creative surge.

Chess:
I love to play chess in my spare time and regularly frequent Twitch chess streams, Chess.com, and Chessable.com. Feel free to shoot me a challenge.

Electronics:
I am also an avid DIYer, especially when it comes to electronics. This interest has been ongoing since childhood, having decided at 10 years old that forgetting my password was a good excuse to take apart my laptop. Though, only recently have I acquired the knowledge to put such a device back together again. Recent projects include building my own desktop computer, configuring a spare CPU and motherboard as a home router, and a few quadcopter. These quadcopter builds inspired further work in this area, leading the way to my M.S. project.

Coding:
I recently worked on a chat bot for Twitch. This bot uses machine learning to classify chat messages into non-offensive and offensive categories to moderate chat in real-time. It is trained on past chat data, uses a linear SVM for classification, and tf-idf for feature vectorization. If you are interested in this project shoot me an email.
